Boards 01

UI-MOB-FAVORITES-MOVEPhone · Fold

A pinned folder moves into Favorites and keeps its details

Why. Today a pinned folder is drawn twice, once in Favorites without its file count and size and once in its own section with them, so the list repeats itself and the pinned row tells less than the one it duplicates. On a phone, where space is the scarce thing, a favorite should move up and leave its old place, and read exactly like the row it replaced. Mobile only: the desktop sidebar and the Fold drawer are untouched.

Unpinning a folder, with the long press or Edit, puts its row back in its section and the section reappears if it was empty. Favorites keeps its order: ordinary folders, photo folders, then audio folders, alphabetical inside each group, places joining their folder’s group.

The cap. A folder must never vanish from both lists, so a pinned folder is always shown and counts toward the five rows; places inside folders fill what is left, in order, and Show all (6 here) reveals the rest. Drawn: three pinned folders and three places, so two places show and Lex Fridman Podcast waits behind Show all. With five or more pinned folders, no place shows until Show all.

Open question. Should a pinned folder that is syncing, in conflict or incomplete show its state in Favorites? Recommendation, drawn above: yes. The row is the Folders row, so documents keeps its progress ring while it syncs and a conflict keeps its tinted tile and word; hiding them would take the state away from a folder that moved. Alternative: a plain tile in Favorites, state only inside the folder.

Accept The phone and the Fold Folders screens draw a pinned folder only in Favorites with the same detail line as its former row, a place inside a folder keeps its location line, folders are never hidden by Show all, Photos or Audio vanish when their folders are all pinned, Edit and the drawer behave as today, and the pages and SPEC’s Favorites paragraph show the new look.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
