Boards 01

UI-SIDEBAR-IDENTITY

The device and its role live in the status card

Desktop & web · Phone · Fold

Why. Under the Arca lockup sits a bordered surface card with a monitor glyph and the device name. It is the boldest box above the nav, yet it is plain text: no click, no hover, nothing to switch, because the app always manages its own daemon and the web always manages the machine that served it. Its glyph is a monitor even on the Linux hub; the role shows only on a hub (“casa … Hub”); offline and service stopped never reach it, they live in the status card at the bottom. In the 64 px rail it becomes an empty bordered box, and the wordmark and status text spill out of the column. The Fold’s wide navigation repeats the card; the phone names itself only in Settings › Device name. The device belongs with the state it is in: “macbook-pro · Replica · Offline” is one fact. A name line in the lockup, a chip beside the wordmark and a device switcher were considered and set aside.

Recommendation. B with the role as a mute word (a). The top becomes brand only, Search is the first interactive row and Favorites stay the loudest list; identity and state read together in one card on desktop, web, phone and Fold; the hub, a server replica and a phone each get a true glyph and a role, not only the hub.

Technical. Desktop and web: index.html deletes .managing and adds a first row in .status-card (glyph, #managed-name, #managed-role); updateShell fills the glyph (status.role === "hub" or web without native → server, else monitor), the name and “Hub”/“Replica”, and gives the card an aria-label “name, role, state” for the rail. style.css deletes .managing rules, moves #sync-controls to grid row 2, and in the 480 px rule hides .brand > span, #connection text, #last-sync, the controls and the backup line, leaving the glyph with a state dot (today .brand b and #connection keep their own size and spill). The tray (tray.js) is unaffected. Phone: Settings (App.jsx) renders a This device row with the existing MachineRow styles and s.caption, and drops the Last completed sync card. Fold: Navigation (components.jsx) drops s.identity (theme.js) and renders the status block at its foot (wide: name, then “Replica · state”, then last sync) or glyph + stateDot (compact); FavoritesDrawer renders the same block at its foot. design/desktop.html and mobile.html redraw after it ships.

Accept No identity card under the lockup on desktop, web or Fold; the lockup is the tile and “arca” only. The status card’s first row shows the kind glyph (server for the hub and a server replica’s web, monitor for the desktop app, smartphone for phones), the name and a mute “Hub” or “Replica”, and is absent before the first status. Up to date, syncing, paused, offline, service stopped and needs attention change only the state line and controls. Below 760 px the name ellipsises and the role stays; in the 64 px rail nothing spills: the card is the glyph with a state dot and an accessible name “name, role, state”. Phone Settings opens with This device (glyph, name, Replica, state · last sync) above Device name, and Local synchronization no longer lists Last completed sync. The Fold’s wide navigation and Favorites drawer end with the same block (name, then “Replica · state”, then last sync), its rail with glyph and dot. The tray is unchanged. Light and dark use tokens only.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
