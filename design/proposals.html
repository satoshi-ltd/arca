Proposals

Purely visual ideas, drawn with the kit's own classes, only while they are proposals. A board is the proposal: there is no ROADMAP entry until the maintainer approves it.

Boards 02

UI-STREAMING-TAGDesktop

A streamed library carries a Streaming tag instead of “Streams from hub”

Why. “· Streams from hub” is the longest part of the line, reads like one more fact about the files, and pushes the useful part (size, or what plays offline) off narrow windows. How the library reaches this device is a property of the row, like a device's role: a tag on the right says it at a glance and leaves the line for the state.

Accept On Folders, every started Audio or Movies & TV library that streams shows a STREAMING tag (the Devices role tag) before its chevron, and its line drops “· Streams from hub”, online and offline alike; a kept library and ordinary folders carry no tag; the row's accessible name still says it streams.

UI-MOB-STREAMING-TAGPhone · Fold

Streamed libraries on the phone carry the same Streaming tag

Why. On the phone a streamed Movies & TV library ends its line in “Streams from hub” and a streamed Audio library says so only until something is downloaded, after which it looks like a kept folder. One tag says how every such library arrives, the same way on the desktop.

Accept On Android, iPhone and the Fold, every started Audio or Movies & TV library that streams shows a STREAMING tag (the Devices role tag) before its chevron, its caption drops “Streams from hub”, and with the hub away it keeps the tag after “Offline · …”; kept libraries carry none; the wording and placement match board UI-STREAMING-TAG.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair. Phones sit side by side; desktop, web and Fold drawings stack Now above Proposed so each keeps its full width.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
