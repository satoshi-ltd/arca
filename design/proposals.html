Boards 01

UI-MOB-GALLERY-MOMENTS

The phone gallery reads as days too

Phone · Fold

Why. This is the phone and Fold counterpart of UI-GALLERY-MOMENTS: the same grouping, drawn for touch. Today the phone timeline is one uniform wall under month headings, with pinch levels of four (six on the Fold) columns, ten (twelve) columns and Years. The proposal turns the base level into days: each group has a readable heading and count, a busy day opens with a hero tile two columns wide, sparse days fold into one quiet row, and a strip of cards brings back the same calendar day from earlier years. The pinch levels become named: Days is the new base level, Months is today's dense grid with month headings, and Years stays as it is; a small pill names the level while a pinch is in progress and fades a second after, because there is no hover to discover it. What differs from the desktop board: a phone has no rail, so the existing fast-scroll thumb and its month bubble stay; the Fold puts On this day in a sticky side column, as SPEC does for two-column layouts, and offers the three levels as a segmented control in the header so they are reachable without a two-finger gesture. Only capture dates are used: no places and no faces, and nothing leaves the device. It needs a per-day count and an index of dates for On this day from the same hub gallery index the months already come from, falling back to the phone's own files offline.

Accept

On the phone and the Fold, the gallery's base level groups photos by capture day under a heading such as "Saturday 12 September" with its count; a day of eight or more photos opens with a hero tile spanning two columns and two rows (three by two on the Fold's six columns), followed by the rest in the usual square tiles with 4 dp gaps; consecutive days of fewer than four photos share one Quiet days group with its date range and count. Months is the existing ten- or twelve-column level with month headings and Years is unchanged; each inward pinch still advances one level and spreading reverses it, one level per gesture, anchored as today. While a pinch is in progress a pill names the level (Years, Months or Days) and fades one second after the gesture ends; accessibility exposes the three levels as actions. On this day shows up to three cards, newest year first, each with the year and the count of photos taken on today's calendar day in an earlier year; a card opens that day in the viewer's sequence, the strip is hidden when there are none, and on the Fold the cards sit in a side column that sticks while the timeline scrolls. The Fold header carries the Years / Months / Days segmented control. The fast-scroll thumb, its month bubble and year chips, selection by long press and the viewer are unchanged; day headings never move what is being viewed when rows load above the viewport. Dates come from the same capture date the months use, the day is the device's local day, the offline gallery builds the groups from the phone's own files, and no place or face data is read. Tiles keep the media placeholder token for unfetched photos, controls keep 48 dp targets, only transform and opacity animate and reduced motion removes the pill's fade; light, dark and 392 dp layouts do not clip.

UI-MOB-MOTION-SYSTEM

Motion on the phone and the Fold

Phone · Fold

Why. On the phone and the Fold motion is a 30 dp push, sheets that slide or fade-scale and a viewer that fades, so opening a photo, a folder or the player reads as a cut and a touch only tints. This is the phone counterpart of the desktop motion in SPEC, with the same job, showing where a thing came from, plus the touch moments: the cell grows into the viewer, the folder tile travels into the header during the push, the mini player's cover grows into Now playing, an edge swipe moves the screen with the finger and a pull on Folders draws the arch mark and starts a cycle. The Fold keeps its rail and single column (list and detail replace each other beside the rail), so it gets the shared elements, a tint that slides down the rail and a dialog that grows from the mini cover. Only transform and opacity animate. It bends SPEC's Mobile and Fold motion rules: tabs no longer only cross-fade, the viewer no longer keeps only its fade, Now playing no longer only slides, and Folders gains a pull gesture and an edge swipe that need native predictive-back support.

Accept

The mini player's cover grows into Now playing's cover on phones and into the dialog's cover on the Fold, over motion-shared 280 ms, falling back to today's slide and fade when it cannot be measured. An edge swipe from the left moves the screen with the finger and completes past a third; Android Back and the Back button keep today's push from the left; this needs predictive back enabled in the native build and a device check, because the Android system gesture owns that edge. Pulling Folders down past 56 dp draws the arch outline with the pull, rings while syncing and settles filled (a standard pull-to-refresh already starts the same cycle). On the phone the folder tile travels into a header tile that does not exist yet. Every transition is opacity or transform, reduced motion removes all of them, and light, dark and 760 px layouts do not clip. SPEC's Motion section (Mobile and Fold bullet) is extended with each moment as it ships.

UI-BRAND-ARCH

The arch as Arca's visual language

System

Why. The mark is an arch with a door, and the app barely uses it: empty states are a dashed box with one muted glyph, a cover without art shows a note, and "Up to date" is a check mark, so every surface looks like the same utility screen. The proposal reuses the mark's own geometry as a quiet motif: one arch outline for every empty state with the context's glyph in its doorway, an arch silhouette for covers without art, an arch hero on the welcome page, and a status seal whose door fills in when sync settles. It is static line art in existing tokens (tint, green, line and the mark's own door colour), with no new logo and no new illustration style. It needs no new data. Gallery placeholders keep the media glyph SPEC requires.

Accept

Every empty state on desktop, web and phone draws the arch with its current glyph in the doorway; titles, texts and actions stay and the dashed border goes. Music covers and album tiles without art show the arch silhouette; gallery placeholders keep the image and play glyphs. The sidebar and phone status show the seal: outline while syncing or offline, door filled when up to date, door in the warning colour for conflicts. The welcome page opens on the arch hero. The arch is one shared inline SVG from the mark's path, aria-hidden, drawn with existing tokens only and identical in light and dark; a seal change is a 200 ms opacity change, none under reduced motion. SPEC's Placeholders and Foundations bullets are updated.

UI-MOB-BRAND-ARCH

The arch on the phone and the Fold

Phone · Fold

Why. UI-BRAND-ARCH sets the arch as Arca's visual language and draws one phone empty state. The phone has more places for it than desktop: the welcome page opens on a 58 px logo, five empty states are a dashed box with one muted glyph, the Status cell of a folder says "Up to date" in plain text, the splash is a static mark, and albums without art show a note on grey, on the phone and on Android Auto. The proposal reuses the mark's own geometry: an arch hero on the welcome page, the same arch with each screen's glyph in its doorway for empty states, a seal in the Status cell whose door fills when the folder settles, a splash that draws the arch in while the app initialises, and the arch silhouette as the cover fallback (on Android Auto it keeps the green art tile and draws the arch in white and lime for legibility at a glance). The Fold gets the centred welcome flow and the explorer's empty state. No new data, no new logo; the launcher, themed and notification icons are unchanged and shown only so the silhouette reads as the same shape. Gallery placeholders keep the image and play glyphs SPEC requires.

Accept

Every phone and Fold empty state (Folders, Files, Recent, History, a file's history, Devices, the photo grid and the music library) draws the arch with its current glyph in the doorway, keeps its title, text and Retry, and loses the dashed frame; prose prompts keep their text. The welcome page replaces the 58 px logo with the arch hero, on the phone and in the Fold's centred flow. The folder Status cell shows a seal beside its words: outline while syncing or offline, door filled when up to date, door and edge in the warning colour for a conflict; the word stays, so colour never carries the meaning alone. The splash shows the arch drawn in three layers cross-fading in 600 ms (outline, filled, door lit), held through initialisation as today, and a static final mark under reduced motion; dark paper keeps the cream arch and lime door. Albums and tracks without art show the arch silhouette on the placeholder token on the phone; on Android Auto and in the media notification the green art tile stays and the arch replaces its glyph, in white with the lime door; gallery tiles keep the image and play glyphs. The launcher, themed and notification icons do not change. The arch is one shared SVG from the mark's path, aria-hidden, drawn with existing tokens and identical in light and dark. SPEC's EmptyState primitive, Mobile screens, Launcher and splash and Placeholders bullets are updated and the native build is needed for the splash and the Android Auto cover.

UI-ROW-RHYTHM

One rhythm for rows

System

Why. Rows in lists do not share a spacing. Folder cards sit 12 px apart with 10/12 padding, Device rows sit 8 px apart with 14/16, History rows share one card with dividers and 10/12, Settings rows use 12/14 and file rows 10/14, so moving between screens the list breathes at a different pace each time and the same card looks taller in one place than in another. The proposal names two row kinds and gives each one measure: card rows (a card per item) are 12 px apart with 12/16 padding, and grouped rows (items sharing one card) are divided by a hairline with the same 12/16 padding; the label above any list sits 8 px over its first row and lists are 24 px apart. Only spacing changes: no colour, type or content.

Accept

Tokens --row-gap 12 px, --row-padding-y 12 px, --row-padding-x 16 px and --section-gap 24 px replace --list-row-padding-*, the 14/16 of Device rows (and their 8 px margin), the 12/14 of Settings rows and the 10/14 of file rows; Folder cards, Device and Music rows, Settings, History and file lists use them, and a list label sits 8 px over its first row. A test asserts no row class in style.css declares its own vertical padding or gap outside the tokens. SPEC's design section names the two row kinds. The mobile counterpart is UI-MOB-ROW-RHYTHM.

UI-MOB-ROW-RHYTHM

One rhythm for rows on the phone and the Fold

Phone · Fold

Why. The same drift exists on touch: folder rows pad 10/12, machine rows 12/16 (14/16 on the Fold), Settings rows 16/16 on the phone and 10/14 on the Fold, action rows 8/16 and cards 16 all round with a 12 gap, so the lists do not line up between tabs. The proposal applies the desktop measures in dp: card rows 12 dp apart and 12/16 inside, grouped rows with hairlines and 12/16, a 48 dp minimum height for every tappable row, a label 8 dp over its first row and 24 dp between lists. The Fold uses the same numbers; only its column width differs.

Accept

design-tokens.js gains rowGap 12, rowPaddingY 12, rowPaddingX 16 and rowMinHeight 48 (mirrored in mobile-tokens.css by npm run design) and replaces listRowPaddingY/X, the machine, setting and action row paddings; the Fold reads the same tokens. A test asserts no row style in theme.js declares its own vertical padding. SPEC's mobile design section names the two row kinds. Light, dark and 760 px layouts do not clip.

UI-MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Small controls that answer when you touch them

System

Why. Beyond route changes, dialogs and the selected segment, small controls change state with a cut: a toggle jumps, Copy swaps its label, a menu is simply there, counts are replaced, a status pill changes colour in one frame and a tick appears. None of them is wrong, but together they make the app feel static where Arca means to feel calm and alive. The proposal gives each small state change one short move of opacity or transform (no layout property): 120 ms for controls (`--motion-fast`), 200 ms for status (`--motion-enter`), one ease, nothing that repeats or loops, and no shake anywhere: errors are shown with colour and text, never movement. Reduced motion shows only the end state.

Accept

Toggle, Copy, dropdown and details menus, counts (filter counts, folder and file totals), status pills, card hover, checked rows and the busy-to-done resolution each animate only opacity and transform over --motion-fast (120 ms) or --motion-enter (200 ms, status) with the shared ease; a count change never moves the layout; Copy returns to its rest label after 1.5 s; reduced motion shows the end state at once; a test per control asserts its keyframes or transitions animate no layout property and that reduced motion zeroes them. SPEC's Motion section lists each. No new tokens beyond the existing ones.

UI-MOB-MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Small touches on the phone and the Fold

Phone · Fold

Why. The phone has the same cuts: switches jump, a long press only draws the tick, Sync now finishes without a sign, status chips and counts change in one frame and the sheet handle never reacts to the finger. The proposal answers each touch with one short move of opacity or transform: the switch knob slides, a long press settles the tile to .94 and grows the tick in, Sync now turns its icon into a tick and back, pills cross-fade, counts roll 4 dp and the sheet handle widens while dragged. Under reduced motion only the end state shows, and nothing shakes.

Accept

Switch, long-press selection, Sync now completion, status pill, count and sheet handle use only transform and opacity over motion.fast (120 ms) or motion.enter (200 ms) with the shared ease, through Animated with the native driver; reduced motion collapses them to the end state; the Fold shares them and has no rail motion of its own beyond the tint slide that already ships; tests prove no layout property animates and the reduced-motion path. SPEC's mobile Motion section lists each.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
