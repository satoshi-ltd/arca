Proposals

Purely visual ideas, drawn with the kit's own classes, only while they are proposals. A board is the proposal: there is no ROADMAP entry until the maintainer approves it.

Boards 01

UI-MENU-SURFACEDesktop

The folder dropdown is drawn from the same family as the ⋯ menu

Why. Two floating lists sit side by side in the same toolbars and look like different products: the ⋯ menu has generous 8 px padding, the large surface corners and no outline; the dropdown has a hairline border inside its shadow, tight 4 px padding, sharper corners and tiny-radius rows. A dropdown is still a select: it keeps the trigger's width, the green selected row and the check on the right.

Accept Every dropdown (the History folder filter, Settings selects) and the tray's ⋯ menu draw with the ⋯ menu's frame: no border beyond the shadow ring, `--r-surface` corners, 8 px padding, `--r-control` rows; a dropdown keeps the trigger's minimum width, the selected row's tint and the trailing check, and its keyboard focus uses the shared 2 px ring; the tray's literal 18 px line height goes; the System page draws the same dropdown.

Format 01

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its ID (UI-<TASKID> when it is the screen of a split task). It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair. Phones sit side by side; desktop, web and Fold drawings stack Now above Proposed so each keeps its full width.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board and its ui line are deleted together, in the change that ships the code, the updated pages, the changelog and SPEC. A task that mixes logic and a screen splits: the screen is board UI-<TASKID> with its ui line, the logic keeps <TASKID> and its accept says the interface follows that board; a board ID never equals a non-ui task ID.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
