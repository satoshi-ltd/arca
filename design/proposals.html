Proposals

Purely visual ideas, drawn with the kit's own classes, only while they are proposals. A board is the proposal: there is no ROADMAP entry until the maintainer approves it.

Boards 01

UI-DESK-FORM-HEIGHTSDesktop

Dialog fields and buttons share the 32 px control height

Why. Fields in dialogs are taller than every other control: a plain input or select is 36 px (9 px padding), a field with an icon about 35 px, and dialog buttons 34 px (8 px padding) while the same buttons elsewhere, pickers and the window's own toolbars are 32 px. Side by side the difference shows, and a dialog's actions look heavier than the page behind it. The proposal puts fields, pickers and dialog buttons on --control-height.

Accept Inputs, selects, fields with an icon (plain and mono) and dialog action buttons measure --control-height (32 px) in every dialog, with text vertically centred and no extra vertical padding; a DOM test reads the computed heights of Create shared folder, Rename and Start syncing.

UI-DESK-DEVICE-ROWSDesktop

Device rows never leave the role and actions on a line of their own

Why. At the 960 px minimum window the Devices list is 712 px wide, but a row needs about 776 px on the hub (name up to 240 px, version, folders and seen cells of 72, 80 and 136 px, the role and actions): the role and the ⋯ menu drop to a line of their own, and the compact layout only starts at 580 px. Names are also cut at 240 px when there is room, and “Seen Oct 7, 2025 at 10:01” overflows its 136 px cell. The proposal lets the name take the free space and switches to the two-line layout before anything wraps.

Accept Between the minimum window and the widest one, a device row is either one line (name taking the free space with no 240 px cap) or the two-line layout with the cells under the name; the role and the ⋯ menu never wrap alone; the seen cell fits its longest date; a DOM test renders the hub list at 712 px and at 1200 px.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair. Phones sit side by side; desktop, web and Fold drawings stack Now above Proposed so each keeps its full width.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
