Proposals

Purely visual ideas, drawn with the kit's own classes, only while they are proposals. A board is the proposal: there is no ROADMAP entry until the maintainer approves it.

Boards 01

UI-DESK-STATUS-CARDDesktop

The sidebar status card keeps its state on one line

Why. The state shares its row with Pause sync and Sync now, so “Conflicts to review”, “Needs attention” and “Checking hub…” get about 63 px and wrap to two lines, and the card grows and shrinks as the state changes. The two buttons are 28 px (an override of the 32 px rule) and the card mixes 14 and 16 px icons. The proposal keeps the controls at the right but moves them one row down: the state gets the full width, and the controls sit beside a shorter last-sync line (a clock and “2 min ago”, the full wording in its tooltip), which fits where “Last sync 2 min ago” would not.

Accept In every state the status card shows its state on one line across the card; the next row shows a clock with the relative last-sync time on the left and Pause sync and Sync now on the right at --button-small-height, with “Last sync …” as the tooltip; the device, state, clock, control and backup icons use one size; the card keeps the same height while the state changes.

UI-DESK-MINI-PLAYERDesktop

The mini player gives the title more room and stays one row

Why. In the 200 px sidebar the mini player fits a 40 px cover, a 28 px play button and a 24 px Next button beside the title, leaving about 58 px — nine characters of “#386 · Cómo cambiará la IA el trabajo”. The sidebar has icon buttons at 24, 28 and 32 px. The proposal keeps it a one-row mini player with its control at the right: a 32 px cover, the title, and one 32 px Play/Pause; Next appears in place while the pointer is over the card or focus is inside it, and stays in the full player and the media keys. The title gets about 90 px, half again as much.

Accept The mini player stays one row: a 32 px cover, the title and subtitle, and Play/Pause at the right as a --button-small-height icon button; Next shows at Play/Pause's left only while the card is hovered or has focus, and is reachable by keyboard; the title shows about 14 characters in the 200 px sidebar instead of 9; the progress line and the card's click target stay as today.

UI-DESK-FORM-HEIGHTSDesktop

Dialog fields and buttons share the 32 px control height

Why. Fields in dialogs are taller than every other control: a plain input or select is 36 px (9 px padding), a field with an icon about 35 px, and dialog buttons 34 px (8 px padding) while the same buttons elsewhere, pickers and the window's own toolbars are 32 px. Side by side the difference shows, and a dialog's actions look heavier than the page behind it. The proposal puts fields, pickers and dialog buttons on --control-height.

Accept Inputs, selects, fields with an icon (plain and mono) and dialog action buttons measure --control-height (32 px) in every dialog, with text vertically centred and no extra vertical padding; a DOM test reads the computed heights of Create shared folder, Rename and Start syncing.

UI-DESK-DEVICE-ROWSDesktop

Device rows never leave the role and actions on a line of their own

Why. At the 960 px minimum window the Devices list is 712 px wide, but a row needs about 776 px on the hub (name up to 240 px, version, folders and seen cells of 72, 80 and 136 px, the role and actions): the role and the ⋯ menu drop to a line of their own, and the compact layout only starts at 580 px. Names are also cut at 240 px when there is room, and “Seen Oct 7, 2025 at 10:01” overflows its 136 px cell. The proposal lets the name take the free space and switches to the two-line layout before anything wraps.

Accept Between the minimum window and the widest one, a device row is either one line (name taking the free space with no 240 px cap) or the two-line layout with the cells under the name; the role and the ⋯ menu never wrap alone; the seen cell fits its longest date; a DOM test renders the hub list at 712 px and at 1200 px.

UI-DESK-FOLDER-METADesktop

Long folder subtitles end with an ellipsis

Why. A folder row's second line is a flex box, so text-overflow: ellipsis never applies: “Path missing · /Volumes/Archive/projects/…” is cut mid-character at the row's edge, and the 5 px flex gap adds a double space after the state word (“2 conflicts · 20 files”). The row already carries the full text as its tooltip, so the line only needs to end cleanly.

Accept Folder row subtitles render as one line of inline text: a line wider than the row ends in “…”, the tooltip shows the whole line, and the state word is followed by a single space before the middle dot.

UI-DESK-SURFACESDesktop

Cards, counters and role badges use one geometry

Why. Settings stacks a Hub connection list with 12 px corners over setting cards with 8 px corners. Counters come in three shapes: the History nav count (11 px, 5 px corners), the History filter counts (10 px, 4 px corners) and the tray's Hub badge, the only grey outlined Hub badge in the app, while tags everywhere else share --tag-height, --r-tag and a filled Hub tone. The proposal moves setting cards to --r-card and draws counters as neutral tags and the tray badge as the filled Hub tag.

Accept Every grouped surface in Settings, Devices and the folder pages uses --r-card; the History nav count, the History filter counts and the tray role badge render as .tag (the counts in the neutral self tone, the badge as tag hub) at --tag-height; the kit's Settings, History and Tray views are updated.

UI-DESK-TYPE-SCALEDesktop

Controls and headings sit on the type scale

Why. Segmented controls use 12 px text and 13 px icons, while the dropdown beside them in History, Files and the music tools and every button use 13 px text and 14–16 px icons, so the two halves of one toolbar read as different sizes. A few headings are off the scale too: the empty-folder message is 12 px regular under its arch while every other empty state is 14 px semibold, and h2 (15 px), stat values (18 px), retention figures (22 px) and photo day headings (16 px) use sizes no token names. The proposal puts segmented controls on --text-control and the small button icon, and every heading on a scale token.

Accept Segmented controls use --text-control and --button-small-icon, matching the dropdown and buttons beside them; the empty-folder message uses the shared empty-state heading; h2, stat values, retention figures and photo day headings use scale tokens; the kit's views are updated.

UI-MOB-NOTICE-DOCKPhone

Notices sit above the tab bar, never over it

Why. Toasts such as “Sync is paused · Resume”, “Saved 3 files” and the photo deletion result are placed 16 dp above the safe area at the root of the app, so they cover the tab bar — and in a music folder the mini player, in a gallery the “N selected · Delete…” bar. The tab you need to leave, or the action you just took, is hidden until the toast goes away.

Accept On the phone a notice stack sits 16 dp above the tab bar, and above the mini player or the photo selection bar while one shows; nothing on those bars is covered; when the bars change height the stack moves with them; component tests measure the stack's bottom against the tab bar.

UI-MOB-FOLD-SPLITSFold

Fold detail pages keep a usable main column

Why. On the unfolded Fold (about 620 dp beside the rail) the two-pane pages keep a fixed side column: a show's poster and facts take 360 dp and leave 236 dp for the episodes, where each row loses 120 dp to its still and the title collapses to “E…” beside the download state; Files keeps a 320 dp side column and leaves 284 dp, and the music track list about 252 dp — narrower than a phone. The proposal makes the side column a share of the width with today's size as its cap, and the episode still 96 dp like the Up next stills.

Accept Below 1100 dp the side column of show, folder and audio-library pages takes 40% of the content width, capped at its current width; the main column is never narrower than 360 dp (the side column stacks above it when it would be); episode stills are 96 dp; episode titles show at least their first line in full on the unfolded Fold.

UI-MOB-RAIL-LABELSFold

Rail labels fit their 60 dp items

Why. The Fold rail is 72 dp with 60 dp items, and its labels use 14 dp semibold on one line: “Favorites” (62 dp) already ends in “Favorit…” at the default text size, Settings and Devices are cut at Large and every label at Largest. A label that changes from screen to screen reads as a glitch, and the rail is the only navigation on the Fold.

Accept Rail labels use the pill size (12/16) with a font-scale cap that keeps them on one line in the 60 dp item; all five labels show whole at the default, Large and Largest text sizes; icons and the selected state stay as today.

UI-MOB-ROW-GEOMETRYPhone

List rows share one lead slot and one height

Why. Rows across the app start their text at different places: 76 dp in Folders (48 dp lead), 72 dp beside image thumbnails, 48 dp beside a bare file icon and 60 dp in History and Devices (32 dp tile), so Files zig-zags between photos and documents. Two-line rows are 66 dp in Files, 70 in History and 74 in Folders and Devices, which SPEC sets for every two-line row; the same 32 dp tile holds a 16 dp glyph in Folders and 20 dp elsewhere; and the Files loading rows draw their own border inside the list's border, then snap when the real rows arrive.

Accept Every list row puts its lead (icon, thumbnail or tile) in one 44 dp slot so titles start at the same x on Folders, Files, History, Devices and Copies; two-line rows are 74 dp; 32 dp tiles hold 16 dp glyphs; loading rows inside a list draw no border of their own; the kit's Files, History and Devices drawings already show this.

UI-MOB-HEADER-TITLEPhone

Folder titles get the full width of the header

Why. The folder and file headers put their three actions (Search, ⋯ and Sync now: 164 dp) on the title's row, so a 392 dp phone leaves about 140 dp for the name: “documents” already wraps on iOS, “Camera uploads 2026” takes three lines and the subtitle three more, and the header grows to about 96 dp, pushing the content down. Truncating alone would still show only “Camera up…”. The proposal moves the actions to the right end of the back row, which is empty today, so the title and subtitle get the whole width.

Accept On the phone the folder and file headers show the back link at the left and Search, ⋯ and Sync now at the right of the same row; the tile, title and subtitle take the full width of the row below; titles take one line and end in “…” only when the full width is not enough (file titles two lines); the subtitle wraps only at “·”; the Fold layout stays as today.

UI-MOB-LARGE-TEXTPhone

Large text never clips or cuts labels

Why. Arca's text size multiplies the phone's own, with no cap, and a few controls keep fixed sizes: the music library's five tabs (about 69 dp each) show “Podca…” and “Playli…” at Large, video badges keep a fixed 20 dp height so HD, 5.1 and language chips clip at Largest, and on the Fold the History date column stays 90 dp so “10:01 PM” wraps while the revision column grows. The proposal lets these grow or scroll instead.

Accept At Large and Largest text the music library tabs scroll sideways with whole labels when they do not fit; video badges grow with their text (minimum --m-tag-height); the Fold History date column scales like the revision column; a font-scale cap keeps the combined app and system scale within the layout; component tests render each at the largest scale.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair. Phones sit side by side; desktop, web and Fold drawings stack Now above Proposed so each keeps its full width.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
