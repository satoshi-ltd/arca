Proposals

Purely visual ideas, drawn with the kit's own classes, only while they are proposals. A board is the proposal: there is no ROADMAP entry until the maintainer approves it.

Boards 01

UI-SYS-DARK-ELEVATION

Dark surfaces lift off the page and show their selection

System

Why. Dark keeps the light theme's shadows, so a menu opened over a card has no visible edge against it, and the menu's buttons look like boxes inside it. The selected segment of a segmented control is the same colour as its track on desktop, and darker than its track on the phone in dark. The proposal gives floating surfaces a raised colour and a one-pixel ring in dark, and gives the selected segment a visible fill in both themes.

Accept

In dark, a menu, a dialog and a phone sheet over a card show a visible edge and a raised surface, and menu buttons are transparent inside the menu; the selected segment is distinct from its track in light and dark on desktop and phone (two new tokens mirrored in the phone palette); light floating surfaces are unchanged; a contrast test covers both.

UI-SYS-HISTORY-DAYS

The day heads the group, the row tells the time

System

Why. History repeats the day in every row ("2 min ago" under a header that already names the day), the string does not fit the time column, and the group headers read "Oct 3, 2026" while the kit draws "Today". The proposal makes the header carry the day and the row carry the clock time.

Accept

Group headers read Today, Yesterday or a short date (rows older than a day show "Oct 1, 14:00" under "Oct 1, 2026" today) (the year only when it is not the current one); every row time is HH:mm in tabular figures; the revision column aligns across rows; desktop and phone match; JSDOM and phone tests cover the labels.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
