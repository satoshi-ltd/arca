Boards 01

UI-DEVICES-TABLEDesktop & web · Phone · Fold

The same Devices rows, their details in aligned columns

Why. Every device row packs OS, an occasional version, folders and size into one dotted line, so each segment starts wherever the previous one ended. Comparing devices means reading every line to find the version or the size, and an outdated client only shows up when its line happens to include a version. Tabulating those details inside the existing rows, as Tailscale’s Machines page does, lets the eye run down a column. The list, the rows, the tiles, the names and the right-edge actions and role chips stay as they ship.

Technical. No new endpoint, table or report field. /v1/machines already returns per device platform, version, selectedFolders, indexedBytes, revoked and lastContact (devices.last_seen, written on every authenticated replica request), and the stored report keeps reportedAt. Desktop deviceRow keeps its markup and roleChip; deviceLine keeps only the OS (and the hub’s Tailscale and address) and the version, folders and last seen move into a fixed-width column group before .device-end, which gets a fixed width per page. The version is always shown, warning-tinted only when older than the hub’s. Last seen, if kept: “Connected” when lastContact is under 120 s old (the window freshness already uses), else “Seen” and its date and time in the Hub backup style; the hub row on a replica uses the replica’s last successful hub request, and with the hub unreachable no other row reads Connected. Mobile MachineRow keeps its title line and RoleChip and replaces segments with the columns. In windows 760 px wide or less the row end moves to its own line as it does today, and the columns stay together after the name.

Accept Devices on desktop, web, phone and Fold keeps its shipped list group and rows: tile, name with This device, and at the right edge the action, Removed or Invitation only pill and the role chips exactly as today. The name’s line shows only the OS (the hub adds Tailscale and its address). Version (with the up-arrow, warning ink and tooltip when older than the hub’s), folders over size, and last seen (if approved) sit in columns that align across every row of the list on desktop and web, in one line under the name on the Fold, and in two columns under the name on the phone. A removed device never shows the up-arrow; an unreported value leaves its column empty, never 0 or a placeholder. Nothing ellipsizes. Light and dark use tokens only. No header bar, table, count pill or new control is added.

Open questions. 1. Last seen: keep the column, and is “Connected” / “Seen Oct 7, 10:01” the wording, or “Seen 3 h ago” under a day? 2. Address: the hub row only (proposed), or a column for every device’s last address on desktop and the Fold.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done. A mobile board adds a Fold pair beside its phone pair.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
