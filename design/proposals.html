Boards 01

UI-MUSIC-FOLDER

A folder becomes a music library

Desktop

Why. Music needs the same decision photos already have: the hub says what a folder is, every device that selects it keeps the whole folder offline as today, and the apps know to open it as a library rather than a file list. The proposal adds Enable music library next to Enable gallery in the hub's Folder actions menu, with its own confirmation ("Browse this folder's music by artist, album and playlist. Files and synchronization stay the same."), and a music tile on the folder card everywhere; a folder is a gallery or a music library, never both. The alternative, detecting audio files automatically, would turn every folder with a podcast or a voice memo into a library.

Accept

On the hub, an ordinary folder's Folder actions menu offers Enable music library with a confirmation; a music folder shows the music tile in Folders and its detail header on desktop, web and phone, and offers neither Enable gallery nor Enable music library; replicas never offer it. A JSDOM test covers the hub menu and the tile; the marker, catalog and status follow MUSIC-FOLDERS.

UI-MOB-MUSIC-LIBRARY

A music folder opens as a library

Mobile

Why. Today a music folder on the phone is a file list: track files sorted by name, a cover.jpg among them, nothing to play. The proposal opens a music folder as a library with four entries that are also the four tabs the car shows: Artists, Albums, Playlists and Recent; Albums is a cover grid, an album is its track list, and a mini player stays at the bottom of every library screen while something plays, opening the Now playing screen on tap. Everything comes from the phone's complete local copy and a saved copy of the hub's index, so it works offline, and View files keeps the plain list reachable from the folder menu. The alternative, a single flat search box, hides the structure the car needs anyway.

Accept

A music folder opens on a library root with Artists, Albums, Playlists and Recent and their counts; Albums is a three-column cover grid, an artist lists their albums, an album and a playlist list their tracks with number, title and duration, and tapping a track starts the queue from there; a mini player (cover, title, artist, pause, next) sits above the tab bar on every library screen while playing and opens Now playing (large cover, title, artist and album, progress, previous/play/next, shuffle and repeat); the folder's actions menu offers View files; all of it works offline from the local copy. A mobile source test covers the screens; the index, player and queue follow MOB-MUSIC-LIBRARY and MOB-MUSIC-PLAYER.

UI-AUTO-BROWSE

Arca on Android Auto

Auto

Why. The car is where a personal music library pays off, and Android Auto draws the whole interface itself: an app only hands it a browse tree, titles with covers, and a media session. The proposal gives the car four tabs, the same roots as the phone library (Artists → Albums → Tracks, Albums, Playlists, Recent), every row with its cover, and the standard Now Playing with previous, play/pause, next, shuffle and repeat. Nothing plays until the driver picks a track, and the tree is served by the phone's native media service from the local index, so the car can start Arca cold while the phone stays in the pocket. The alternative, a flat list of folders and files, is what Android Auto's quality rules reject (every item needs a title and art, and deep trees fail review).

Accept

With Unknown sources enabled, Android Auto lists Arca; its root offers Artists, Albums, Playlists and Recent as tabs; Artists → Albums → Tracks is at most three levels deep; every item carries a title, a subtitle and a cover or the placeholder art; picking a track plays it and the Now Playing screen shows cover, title, artist, album, progress, previous, play/pause, next, shuffle and repeat; nothing auto-plays on connection; the tree and playback work with the app not running on the phone (cold start from the car) and offline. Kotlin tests cover the tree builder; the service follows MOB-ANDROID-AUTO and the maintainer's VERIFY-ANDROID-AUTO proves it on the DHU and in the car.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
