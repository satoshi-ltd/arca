Proposals

Purely visual ideas, drawn with the kit's own classes, only while they are proposals. A board is the proposal: there is no ROADMAP entry until the maintainer approves it.

Boards 01

UI-RETENTION-ONE-LINE

Retention choices stay on one line

Desktop

Why. The hub's Version history panel offers Off, 1 day, 1 week, 30 days and Forever in the shared segmented control, which wraps and pads every segment 10 px a side: the five need about 287 px, one more than the 286 px the 320 px side column leaves and far more than the 260 or 230 px the web admin leaves on a 390 or 360 px phone, so Forever drops alone to a second row on the desktop and on phones, as in the maintainer's phone screenshot. The phone app has no such control (it only shows the Version history summary), so the fix belongs to the desktop and web panel. The proposal gives this control a fill variant of the same component, like Web access's sign-in tabs: one row that never wraps, segments of equal width with centred labels that never break, and --space-1 of side padding as the floor, so the five need about 224 px and hold from a 354 px viewport. Labels, order and the sentence below stay as they are. The alternative, a "Keep older versions for · 30 days" row opening the shared dropdown, fits any width and longer translated labels, but hides the five choices and their order behind a tap; the segmented row keeps them comparable at a glance, and reductions already confirm.

Accept

On a hub, a folder's Version history panel shows Off, 1 day, 1 week, 30 days and Forever on one row with no label clipped or broken, in the desktop's 320 px side column and in the web admin at 360, 390, 480 and 481 to 760 px viewports; segment padding and type come from tokens (--space-1, --text-body) and the other segmented controls (Files/Recent, history filters, theme, network mode, sign-in) keep their layout. A desktop JSDOM test with style.css asserts the retention group computes as the fill variant (no wrap, equal flexible segments, nowrap labels) while the history filters still wrap; a headless render at a 360 px viewport shows one row; the Desktop view in the design kit draws the new panel.

Format 02

Board

One article per idea, tagged with its task ID. It carries the ID, the area (System, Desktop, Mobile, Auto), a title, why it is worth doing, a "Now" drawing of what exists, a "Proposed" drawing, and what proves it done.

Drawings

Both drawings use production and kit classes only, so the page reads as the app would. A drawing is never a screenshot and never introduces a colour or size outside the tokens.

Lifecycle

Idea: a board only. Approved: one ROADMAP Queue line of type ui with the board ID, whose accept is the board. Shipped: the board is deleted, the changelog and SPEC record it, and this kit is updated.

No visual part

Behaviour, data and infrastructure tasks live only in ROADMAP and never get a board.
