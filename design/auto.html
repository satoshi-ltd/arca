Auto

The Android Auto surface: what Arca shows on a car's head unit while the phone drives it. The car draws its own chrome; Arca only provides the browse tree, the covers and the playback state, drawn here with the kit's tokens.

Head unit 01

Arca appears in Android Auto as a media app once Unknown sources is on in Android Auto's developer settings. The phone's media service serves the tree from its published library without JavaScript, so the car can start Arca cold; every row carries a title, a subtitle and its cover when the phone holds one, and nothing plays until the driver picks a track. The tabs follow the catalog the phone and desktop share: Artists, Albums, Playlists once a playlist has a track on the phone, and Recent. The car never edits: adding, removing, renaming and deleting stay on the phone and desktop.
