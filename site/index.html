02 / Bring your devices together

One hub.
Your devices around it.

One computer or server is your hub: it keeps your shared folders and their history. An always-on machine lets your devices sync without waiting for your laptop to wake up.

  1. 01

    Choose your hub.

    Run Arca on your computer or a server with Docker. Use the web app to manage the server and choose the folders to share.

  2. 02

    Pair your devices.

    Connect with a single-use pairing code. Use your local network, or your own private network such as Tailscale when you’re away.

  3. 03

    Choose your folders.

    Select whole folders independently on each device. Choose a local path on desktop; on mobile, Arca keeps the selected copies in app storage.

  4. 04

    Link your photo albums.

    Link a phone album to upload photos and videos to your hub, without a second Arca copy on your phone. Explore your gallery by date on desktop or the web.

  5. 05

    Set your history retention.

    Choose how long each folder retains earlier versions. Keep more history for ongoing work, or a shorter window for folders that change often.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Do I need a server to use Arca?

A dedicated server is optional. A computer you already own can be the hub, or you can run one with Docker. Phones and tablets connect to a hub; they cannot act as one.

Where do my files live?

On your hub and the devices where you select their folders. Desktop copies use local paths you choose; mobile copies live in app storage. Your own disks set the capacity.

Does every device need to keep everything?

No. Each device chooses whole folders independently. Selected folders download as complete files, ready for offline use. A linked phone album is different: it uploads without keeping a second Arca copy on that phone.

Does my hub have to stay on?

Only to exchange changes. Once a folder finishes syncing, its local files work offline. Edits wait until the hub is reachable again. An always-on hub makes syncing more convenient.

Can I use Arca away from home?

Yes, if your device can reach the hub, for example through your own private network with Tailscale. Arca provides no public relay and requires no external storage account.

How do photo albums work?

Link a phone album to a hub folder. Arca uploads new photos and videos; browse them by date on desktop or the web. Deleting from your phone’s gallery keeps uploaded files, and hub changes never write back to it.

Will my phone sync in the background?

Background work follows iOS and Android scheduling limits. Arca also resumes work when you open it. Keep the app open for a large first upload; background sync is not continuous.

Is sync a backup?

Sync also carries file deletions between ordinary folder copies. Retained history offers a way back, but keep an independent backup. Arca’s optional full hub backup runs on a desktop or server, separately from working copies.

Can I undo an edit or recover a deleted file?

Yes, while its history is retained. Restoring creates a new revision, leaving earlier history intact. Conflicting edits are kept as separate versions for you to review.

How do I update Arca?

Download and install a new version on your devices; update your server deployment separately. Check the release notes for any requirement to update the hub and replicas together.
