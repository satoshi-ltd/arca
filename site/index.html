02 / Keep the storage in your hands

Your files need a home.
You can provide it.

Keeping devices in sync doesn’t have to mean putting your files in a provider’s storage account. With Arca, you supply the storage, choose the devices and decide which folders each one keeps.

Your capacity comes from your own disks. Your files remain ordinary files. Arca needs no external account and runs without a public relay or telemetry.

You also run the hub and look after its storage and backups. Devices need to reach it to exchange changes; completed local copies remain usable while it’s offline.

A few good questions

Know what you’re keeping.

Where do my files live?

On your hub and the devices where you select their folders. Desktop uses local paths you choose; mobile keeps complete copies in app storage. Arca has no external account, public relay or telemetry.

Is sync the same as a backup?

No. File deletions synchronize too. Retained history lets you restore earlier content, while an optional full hub backup on a desktop or server is separate from working copies. Phones cannot be hubs or full-backup devices.

What happens when two devices edit the same file?

Arca preserves conflicting versions separately. Review them and choose which content to keep; restoring creates a new revision rather than rewriting the past.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, when your device can reach the hub over your own private network, for example with Tailscale. Offline copies remain available without a connection. Arca does not provide a hosted relay.

Does a new release update my apps automatically?

This page offers the current published installers. Download and install a new version to update your apps; server deployments are managed by you.
