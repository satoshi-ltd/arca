Arca as a self-hosted Google Photos alternative

Google Photos keeps your pictures on Google’s servers and analyses them: that is how it knows who and what is in each one. Arca keeps the originals on a computer you own. Nobody else stores them and nothing learns from them: your photos, under your rules.

Why choose Arca

  • Your photos stay yours. They live on your hub and your devices and nowhere else. You decide where they are stored, how long they are kept and which devices see them.
  • Originals, never recompressed. Arca uploads the files your camera made, as ordinary files any app can open.
  • No storage plan. Your own disks set how much you can keep, and nothing is billed monthly.
  • Nothing learns from your pictures. Arca does not scan faces or contents, has no account and sends no telemetry.
  • One timeline from every device. Photos from several of your devices land in the same folder, grouped by month.
  • On every screen. Phone, computer, web and TV.

What Arca leaves out, on purpose

Arca shows your photos by date. It does not recognise faces or search by content, because that means analysing your pictures, and it does not edit photos or share albums with other people. A phone with a linked album keeps the complete shared folder, so allow space for it, and the hub is the copy you back up.
