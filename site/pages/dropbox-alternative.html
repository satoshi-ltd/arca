Arca as a self-hosted Dropbox alternative

Dropbox keeps your files on its servers, behind an account and a storage plan. Arca keeps them on a computer you own: you decide where they live, how long their history is kept and which devices reach them. No account, no subscription, and as much space as your disks hold.

Why choose Arca

  • Your data stays yours. Nothing is stored on a company’s servers and nobody else holds a copy. The rules are yours to set.
  • No subscription. Arca is free and open source, and your own disks set the space.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no telemetry.
  • Complete files that work offline. Folders sync both ways between your laptop, phone and other computers.
  • History included. The hub keeps earlier versions for 30 days by default, adjustable per folder.
  • More than a drive. The same hub holds your photos, music and movies.

What Arca leaves out, on purpose

Arca is for your own devices, so it has no sharing links or shared folders for other people. You run the hub and its backups, and away from home a device reaches it through a private network or your own HTTPS address.
