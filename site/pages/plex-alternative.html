Arca as a Plex alternative

Plex asks for an account and charges for some of its features. Arca plays your own movies, shows and music with no account and nothing to pay. Your library and what you watch stay on your hub and your devices, and the same hub also syncs your files and photos.

Why choose Arca

  • Your library stays yours. It lives on your hub, and what you play stays on your hub and your devices. You decide how it is stored and which devices reach it.
  • No account. Nothing signs in to an outside service. With your own TMDB key, only titles and years leave the hub.
  • Nothing to pay. Downloads for a trip, playback away from home and every other feature are free, and the software is open source.
  • Your files as they are. Arca never re-encodes video, so what you watch is what you stored. Audio a device cannot play gets a compatible track.
  • A calm home screen on your TV. arca TV brings Watch next, your photos and your music to Google TV and Android TV, free and with no ads.
  • One hub for everything. Movies and shows with posters and Watch next, music with artists and albums, plus your files and photos.
  • Light on the hub. With no video to convert, the hub has far less work to do.

What Arca leaves out, on purpose

On a television Arca runs on Google TV and Android TV, through arca TV. Because it never converts video, a file a device cannot decode is handed to another app on that device. It has no library sharing with other people and no live TV.
