Your files become a library.

On the hub, choose Set up as library… → Movies & TV on a folder. Arca names movies, shows and episodes from their files, and your own free TMDB key adds posters, ratings and cast.

Pick up where you left off.

Watch next follows you from laptop to phone to TV. On a television, arca TV for Google TV and Android TV plays the library from its own home screen.

Stream, download or keep.

Every device streams from the hub. The phone and the desktop app download single titles for a trip, or keep the whole video folder in sync.

What to know first

  • With a TMDB key, only the titles and years of your library are sent to TMDB. Nothing else leaves your hub.
  • Arca never re-encodes video. When a device cannot play a file’s audio, the hub gives it a compatible audio track; a file whose video a device cannot decode is offered to another app on that device.
  • The hub keeps earlier versions only of video files changed through Arca, and the optional full backup skips video libraries. Keep your own copy of your video files.
  • On a television, Arca runs on Google TV and Android TV, through arca TV.
