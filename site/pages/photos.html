Originals, never recompressed.

Arca uploads the files your camera made to a folder on a computer you run. They are ordinary files any app can open, and your own disks set how much you can keep.

One timeline on every device.

Photos and videos appear grouped by month, newest first, on the phone, the desktop app, the web and arca TV.

Several devices, one folder.

More than one device can add to the same shared folder, so the pictures from two phones end up in one place.

What happens when you delete

  • Deleting a photo from your phone’s own gallery, after it has uploaded, leaves the Arca copy in place.
  • Deleting it inside Arca removes it from that shared folder on every synced device. It stays recoverable from the hub for as long as the folder’s history is kept.
  • Arca never writes to your phone’s photo library and never removes originals from it.

What to plan for

  • A phone with a linked album keeps the complete shared folder in Arca’s storage, including photos from your other devices. Allow space for it.
  • Background uploads follow the limits iOS and Android set. Keep the app open for a large first upload.
  • There is no face recognition, search by content or sharing link for people outside your devices.
