Arca as a Nextcloud alternative

Nextcloud is a platform to install, configure and maintain. Arca is one app: it keeps your folders, photos, music and movies on your own devices, with no platform to run.

Why choose Arca

  • The same ownership, with less work. Your data stays on your own hardware and you set the rules, without running a platform to get there.
  • No platform to administer. The desktop app can be the hub. There is no web server, database or list of apps to keep updated.
  • Everything from one project. Phone, desktop, web and TV apps are made together.
  • Complete files, always. Each device keeps whole copies of the folders it chooses, ready offline, and the hub keeps their history.
  • Libraries built in. A photo timeline, a music library and movies with posters come with Arca, not as add-ons.
  • No accounts. You pair your devices with a code and that is all.

What Arca leaves out, on purpose

Arca is built for one person’s devices. It has no user accounts, sharing links, calendar, contacts or office documents. You still run the hub and its backups. If you need a server for a team, that is a different job.
