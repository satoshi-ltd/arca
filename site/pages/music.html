Your files become a library.

On the hub, choose Set up as library… → Audio on a folder. Arca shows the music you already have by artist, album and what you played recently.

Stream or keep, per device.

A computer keeps the whole folder or streams it from the hub. An Android phone streams and downloads the artists, albums or tracks you choose.

In the car and in the browser.

Android Auto browses and plays the library from your phone, with lock-screen controls. The web plays it from any browser signed in to your hub.

Playlists that are yours

The playlists you make are saved as ordinary playlist files in the music folder, so they sync like any file and still work outside Arca.

What is missing today

  • An iPhone keeps the whole music folder and browses it, but opens tracks in other apps for now. CarPlay is not available.
  • Streaming needs the hub to be reachable. Away from home that means a private network or your own HTTPS address.

How it compares
