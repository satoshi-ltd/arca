Arca as a Syncthing alternative

Syncthing keeps folders in sync between devices that are online at the same time. Arca adds what is missing: a hub that always has everything, its history, and libraries for your photos, music and movies.

Why choose Arca

  • The same ownership, and more on top. Your data stays on your own devices and you set the rules, with a hub that makes it a personal cloud.
  • One place that always has everything. With an always-on hub, your phone and laptop never need to be awake at the same time.
  • History in one place. The hub keeps earlier versions of your folders for 30 days by default, whichever device made the change.
  • More than folders. The same files open as a photo timeline, a music library and a movie library.
  • One app on every screen. Phone, desktop, web and TV, made together.
  • Simple to reason about. Every device talks to the hub. There is no mesh of devices and folder pairs to keep track of.

What Arca leaves out, on purpose

Arca needs one computer to be the hub, and away from home a device reaches it through a private network or your own HTTPS address. Arca has no public relay: you choose the route.
